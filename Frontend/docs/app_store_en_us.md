## Description
PusulaFit helps you build a clear fitness routine without juggling multiple apps.

Track your workouts, log meals, monitor weight changes, follow daily tasks, and get AI-powered guidance based on your progress. Whether your goal is to lose weight, build muscle, or maintain your current shape, PusulaFit gives you a practical plan you can follow every day.

Key features:
- Workout tracking with sets, reps, weights, and progress history
- Nutrition tracking with calories, protein, carbs, fats, water, and meal timing
- Weight progress charts with streaks and goal tracking
- AI coaching for nutrition, weight trends, and daily decisions
- Achievement room with streaks, milestones, and progress badges
- US-friendly food suggestions such as Greek yogurt, oatmeal, turkey wraps, chicken rice bowls, protein bars, and more
- Imperial and metric units: lb, in, fl oz, kg, cm, and L

PusulaFit is designed for real life: quick meals, repeatable workouts, visible progress, and fewer decisions when you are tired.

Premium unlocks deeper AI coaching, advanced planning, and more personalized recommendations.
